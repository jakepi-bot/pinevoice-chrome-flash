Enter BootROM manually
  1. Click “Connect serial port” and pick the device once, so the page is allowed to reopen it.
  2. PineVoice: power off → hold the center touch ring → power on (keep the USB-C cable connected).
  3. The BootROM window is only a few seconds: the page reconnects and latches it the moment the port appears. Keep the ring held until “BootROM latched” shows in the log.
  4. If it still misses, click “Connect serial port” again and power-cycle with the ring held.
